Your UPI safety coach

Remember these, always

  1. Entering your PIN always sends money — never to receive it.
  2. No real bank ever asks you to install AnyDesk or TeamViewer.
  3. Scanning a QR code to "get" money is always a scam.
  4. "Account blocked in 2 hours" + a link = fake, every time.
  5. Only call the number on your card — never one from Google.
