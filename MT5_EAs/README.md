**Setup:** copy the `.mq5` files into MT5 → File → Open Data Folder → MQL5 → Experts. Compile each one (F4 → F7, 0 errors)
and attach each EA to its chart. On every EA set only `InpAccountSize` = your account balance. Turn Algo Trading on.
Before each new account, press F3 and delete all `PP_...` global variables.

`SPX_Breakout_5m` needs a low US500 spread (about 0.5 points or less). Its edge is thin at double costs, so leave it off if your broker's spread is wide.

Full guide and test results: [`strategies/mt5/README.md`](../strategies/mt5/README.md).
These files are generated by `tools/make_mt5.py`; don't edit them by hand.

## Option: one EA with 5+ trades a day – `pack/Breakout_Pack.mq5`
One EA that runs six morning-breakout strategies on four markets (Nasdaq, S&P 500, DAX, gold) from a single chart,
about **5.3 trades per weekday**:

| Module | Market | Timeframe | Risk | Same logic as |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| NQORB | Nasdaq | M5 | 0.625% | NQ_Opening_Range |
| NQBO15 | Nasdaq | M15 | 0.2% | NQ_Breakout_15m |
| NQBO5 (new) | Nasdaq | M5 | 0.05% | 50-bar breakout, EMA800, 2 ATR stop |
| SPXBO5 | S&P 500 | M5 | 0.1% | SPX_Breakout_5m |
| DAXORB | DAX | M15 | 0.2% | DAX_Opening_Range |
| GOLDASIA | Gold | H1 | 0.3125% | Gold_Asia_Breakout |

**Setup:**
1. **Remove these 5 single EAs from their charts:** NQ_Opening_Range, NQ_Breakout_15m, SPX_Breakout_5m, DAX_Opening_Range and Gold_Asia_Breakout.
2. **Keep the other 7:** BTC_Dip_v3, Gold_Dip, Gold_Intraday_Momentum, NQ_Dip_2h, SPX_Dip_1h, Oil_Dip_1h and BTC_Weekend_Gap.
3. Attach `Breakout_Pack` to any one chart (e.g. US100 M5).
4. In its inputs, set:
   - your broker's symbol names (RoboForex defaults: `.USTECHCash`, `.US500Cash`, `.DE40Cash`, `XAUUSD`)
   - `InpAccountSize`
5. Check the Experts tab: a "symbol not found" warning means a symbol name is wrong.

Tested (`tools/pack_addon_test.py`): the pack plus those 7 single EAs is the same as the 12 EAs plus the NQ 5m breakout at 0.05%.
That gives pass 100% / 100% / 100%, 0 breaches, a funded median of +119%, and 2026 at +46%.

At 0.1% the NQ 5m breakout caused small breaches, so keep it at 0.05%. A DAX 5m breakout was rejected: it breached on up to 17% of attempts.

## Option: Gold VWAP Pullback – `optional/Gold_VWAP_Pullback_5m.mq5` (Nov 2026)
A rebuilt, safer version of a friend's 1-minute gold martingale EA. **XAUUSD M5 chart, magic 1122, 0.2% per trade, no martingale.**
Above today's VWAP + RSI(7) oversold (or turning up) + bullish candle → wait up to 10 bars for a 1.8 x ATR pullback → buy on a bullish
bar; mirror for sells. Only 08:00-21:00 UTC and only when ATR(14) ≥ 4 x the spread. Stop 2 x ATR, target 4 x ATR, trail 0.5 x ATR
once 0.8 x ATR in profit. `tools/gold_vwap_pullback_search_test.py`: picked on 2020-22 (+17 R/yr), confirmed on 2023-24 (+15) and
2025-26 (+23); ~233 trades/yr, 73% wins, +0.077R, 7/7 years positive, max drawdown −12.5R; still positive with double spread.
The original 1-minute version with martingale lost in every year 2020-2025. Not yet tested together with the 12 EAs.

## Option: Solana dip – `optional/SOL_Dip_1h.mq5` (Nov 2026) – the best add-on so far
**SOLUSD H1 chart, magic 1119, 0.3% per trade.** Buys when RSI(2) < 15 and the close is above EMA(200), unless SOL fell more than 8%
in 30 days; stop 2 × ATR(14) under the signal close, exit after 144 bars (6 days). About 4-5 trades a month, held through weekends.

## Option: Nikkei breakout – `optional/Nikkei_Breakout_5m.mq5` (Nov 2026)
A new market: **JP225 / .JP225Cash, M5 chart, magic 1118, 0.4% per trade** (with the other 12 EAs turned down, see below). From 09:00 to 11:30 Tokyo, a 5-minute close above the
high of the last 20 bars and above EMA(800) → buy (mirror for sells); stop 2 × ATR(14), no target, flat 14:55 Tokyo.
About 1 trade per weekday, between 00:00 and 05:55 UTC, when the other EAs are quiet. Needs the broker's server clock to be detected
(`InpAutoServerOffset = true`).

| Test | Result |
|---|---|
| Dukascopy 2021-23 (rules picked here) | +24 R/yr, but only +4 R/yr with double costs |
| **Broker data Jun 2025 – Nov 2026 (unseen)** | **+34 R/yr**, +17 R/yr with double costs, 5 of 7 quarters positive |
| Alone, all data | 36% wins, avg +0.12R, max drawdown −24.5R, losing streak 11, 28 of 45 months positive |

**Settings for this mix (Pass Mode):** the 12 core EAs `InpRiskMultiplier = 1.2` (instead of 1.5), Nikkei EA `InpRiskMultiplier = 1.5`

It is a small diversifier, not a game changer. Cost-sensitive: use it only where the Nikkei spread is about 10 yen or less.

## Realistic results under FTMO's actual daily-loss rule (Nov 2026)
FTMO sets the daily floor at **midnight balance (closed trades) − 5% of the starting capital**. Open losses carried past midnight
already use up part of the next day's 5%. The dip EAs often hold losing trades overnight (median about −1% open, a quarter of
the time about −3%). Earlier tests measured the floor from the day-start equity and were too optimistic (100% pass).
Re-tested with FTMO's exact rule (`tools/ftmo_balance_rule_test.py`; an attempt every weekday; the guard closes everything at
−4% with 0.3% slippage):

| Setup | Pass (2020-22 / 2023-25 / 2026) | Breached | Stopped at −9.5% | Median days |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Drawdown brake on the day-start **balance** (previous EAs) | 75 / 86 / 92% | ~0% | 25 / 14 / 8% | 78 / 70 / 58 |
| **Brake on the day-start equity (current EAs)** | **89 / 96 / 98%** | **0%** | 11 / 4 / 2% | 88 / 84 / 59 |
| Same, all risk ×0.75 | 91 / 97 / 99% | 0% | 9 / 3 / 1% | 103 / 102 / 88 |

The room cap and the half-size mode now use `DayStartEquity()`, the equity at the first check of the server day with open
trades included. They slow down while losing trades are still open. The daily guard itself keeps using the day-start
balance, exactly like FTMO. The Monte Carlo figures above were run before this fix and are optimistic.
**Live, expect roughly 85-95% pass and 0 rule breaches. "Stopped" attempts (the EAs halt at −9.5%) still cost the fee.**

### Speed Mode: when days matter more than passing on the first try (Nov 2026)
Buy a new account after each failure and count calendar days and fees until funded (`tools/speed_resets_test.py`;
12 EAs + SOL_Dip_1h at 0.3%, FTMO exact rule, 4-day minimum per phase, a start every 3 days Sep 2020 – Mar 2026, $250 per account):

| Setup | First try | Median days | Funded ≤30 d | ≤60 d | Avg accounts | Avg fees |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Pass Mode (x1.5, room 9) | 98% | 58 | 28% | 51% | 1.02 | $254 |
| **Speed Mode x3** (`InpRiskMultiplier 3`, `InpRoomCapPct 5`) | 67% | **33** | 46% | 73% | 1.5 | $384 |
| Speed Mode x4 (`InpRiskMultiplier 4`, `InpRoomCapPct 5`) | 58% | **29** | 52% | 78% | 1.7 | $435 |
| One trade per phase at 4.5% (BTC/SOL dip, `tools/one_shot_resets_test.py`) | 16% | 35 | 44% | ~70% | 7.4 | $1,860 |

The one-big-trade idea is slower *and* about 4x the fees: dip signals come only ~9 times a month and ~2 in 3 lose at a 2.2R target.
In Speed Mode the EAs' `InpGuardShare` (60% of the remaining daily room per trade) caps the biggest trades, so live results are a bit
slower and safer than the table. Go back to `InpRiskMultiplier 1.0` once funded.

### Pass Mode preset (higher pass rate *and* faster), Nov 2026
Tested with `tools/pass_mode_test.py`: same simulator and FTMO rule as above, 61 sizing settings, an attempt started every weekday.
Only inputs change (every EA, same values); no new code:

| Preset | `InpRiskMultiplier` | `InpRoomCapPct` | `InpDailyStopPct` | Pass (2020-22 / 2023-25 / 2026) | Breached | Median days | 80% done by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Default | 1.0 | 7 | 4 | 89 / 96 / 98% | 0% | 88 / 84 / 59 | 219 / 203 / 108 |
| **Pass Mode (recommended)** | **1.5** | **9** | **4** | **93 / 97 / 98.5%** | **0%** | **76 / 77 / 58** | 258 / 231 / 106 |
| Fast Pass | 2.0 | 9 | 4 | 89 / 95 / 92% | 0-0.6% | 60 / 52 / 41 | 267 / 200 / 92 |
| Too fast (do not use) | 3.0 | 7 | 3 | 69 / 77 / 66% | 3-6% | 28 / 26 / 25 | — |

Pass Mode trades 1.5x bigger while the account is healthy, and the wider room cap (9%) shrinks size earlier and more gently
as it loses. It passes more often than the default and ~10 days sooner. Risking more than 2x does not pass faster
safely: breaches appear and the pass rate falls. No setting in the 61 tested passes ~100% within weeks: the fastest
settings with 0 breaches still take a median of 50+ days.
